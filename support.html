Common questions

How do I play? You're shown one photo of a real place — guess the country. Wrong guesses show the distance and point a compass toward the answer. Solve it in as few guesses as you can.

What is Meridian+? An optional subscription that removes ads, unlocks unlimited practice and the full daily archive, and sends a larger share of revenue to conservation. Manage or cancel anytime in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

How do I restore a purchase? Open Profile → Meridian+ → Restore Purchases.

How do I delete my account? Open Profile, tap your name to open the account screen, then Delete account. This permanently removes your data.

Do I need an account? No — you can play as a guest. Adding an email saves your streak and passport across devices.
