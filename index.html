Meridian

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Meridian is a daily geography guessing game. We care about keeping your data minimal and your play private. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. By using Meridian, you agree to this policy.

Who we are

Meridian ("we", "us") is an independent app. For any privacy question, contact us at jmbray25@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Play data. Your guesses, scores, streaks, solved countries (your "passport"), and which puzzles you've played. This makes the game work and powers leaderboards and stats.
  • Account (optional). You can play as a guest with no account. If you choose to save your progress, we store your email address and a password (handled by our authentication provider). You may also set a display name and a friend code, which are visible to friends and on leaderboards.
  • Purchases. If you subscribe to Meridian+, we receive your subscription status and purchase history from the app store. We never see or store your full payment card details.
  • Advertising & device data. To show ads and keep the game free, our advertising partner may access a device advertising identifier and similar technical data. On Apple devices you'll be asked whether to allow tracking; if you decline, you'll still see ads, just less personalized.
  • Basic technical data. Standard information such as app version and general device type, used to run and improve the app.

We do not collect your contacts, photos, precise location, or browsing history.

How we use it

  • To run the game — your progress, streaks, leaderboards, friends, and passport.
  • To provide and manage Meridian+ subscriptions.
  • To show ads that keep the free version running.
  • To fix problems and improve the experience.

We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

We rely on a few trusted services to operate Meridian. Each receives only the data needed for its role:

  • Supabase — secure database and account hosting for your play data and account.
  • Apple — app distribution and in-app subscription billing.
  • RevenueCat — manages and validates subscription status.
  • Google AdMob — serves ads in the free version.
  • Stripe — processes subscription payments for the web version, if used.

These providers process data under their own privacy policies.

Your choices & rights

  • Delete your account. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data from inside the app, under your profile's account screen. This cannot be undone.
  • Play without an account. Guest play stores progress on your device and our servers under an anonymous id, with no email required.
  • Ad tracking. You control ad tracking through your device's privacy settings and the on-device prompt.
  • Access or questions. Email us any time to ask what we hold or to request deletion.

Data retention

We keep your play data while your account is active. When you delete your account, the associated data is removed from our database. Anonymized or aggregated data that can't identify you may be retained.

Children

Meridian is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we'll remove it.

Conservation donations

Meridian donates a share of its revenue to conservation organizations. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by those organizations; donation information is provided for transparency and does not involve sharing your personal data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected by the "last updated" date above.
